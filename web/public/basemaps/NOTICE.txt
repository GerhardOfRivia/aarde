Made with Natural Earth. Public-domain map data.
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/

Source: https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/tree/v5.1.2/geojson
Pinned release: v5.1.2. Scale: 1:110 million.

Inputs (download from the source above, saving under these local names):
  ne_110m_admin_0_countries.geojson -> countries.geojson
  ne_110m_admin_1_states_provinces_lines.geojson -> states.geojson
  ne_110m_populated_places_simple.geojson -> places.geojson

To regenerate from the web directory:
  node scripts/prepare-basemap.mjs /path/to/downloaded/inputs

Aarde retains geometry and selected label properties, rounds coordinates to four
decimal places, and creates country label points from Natural Earth's label
coordinates. The state boundary dataset at this scale covers the United States.
Country boundaries follow Natural Earth's default de facto representation.
This is a generalized overview map, without roads or street-level detail.

The resulting GeoJSON and this notice are committed to the repository and copied
into the frontend build, then embedded in the Go binary and container image.
Normal builds and map use do not download Natural Earth data.
